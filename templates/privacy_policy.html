Privacy Policy

Khzanti · Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Khzanti ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Name, username, email address and password (stored securely on our servers).
  • Email verification codes used to confirm your account or reset your password.

Profile and style information

  • Details you choose to provide, such as age, gender, height, body type and country.
  • Your profile picture.

Photos and images

  • Photos you take with your camera or select from your photo library to create your avatar, add wardrobe items or make posts.
  • Avatar, outfit and try-on images generated from your photos.

Content you create

  • Wardrobe items, saved outfits, posts, captions and outfit ratings.
  • People you follow and people who follow you.

Information stored on your device

  • Your login session and language preference, kept so you stay signed in and see the app in your chosen language.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not contain third-party advertising or analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Generate your avatar, outfit suggestions and virtual try-on images.
  • Show your shared outfits and posts to other users, and let others rate them.
  • Show you outfits and posts from people you follow ("Friends Style").
  • Send verification and password-reset emails.
  • Keep the app secure and fix problems.

3. Camera and Photo Library Access

The app asks for permission before using your camera or photo library. We use:

  • Camera: to take photos for your avatar, wardrobe or posts.
  • Photo library (read): to let you choose existing photos.
  • Photo library (save): to save avatars, wardrobe items and outfits to your device when you tap download.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. AI Image Processing

To create avatars, outfits and try-on images, your photos are sent to our servers and processed by AI image generation technology. Our servers use the following third-party AI services to process your data: fal.ai (image generation) and OpenAI (outfit suggestions, which use profile details such as gender, age and country together with your wardrobe items). These providers process data only to generate results for you and do not use it for advertising.

5. What Other Users Can See

  • Your username, name, profile picture, and any posts or outfits you mark as shared or public are visible to other users.
  • Ratings on your shared content are visible as averages.
  • Content you keep private is not shown to other users.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the app (such as hosting, image storage and AI image processing), and only as needed to provide the service.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the app.

7. Payments

If you buy a subscription, payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on our servers at khzanti.online and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app, from your Profile menu. When you delete your account, your personal information, photos and content are removed from our servers, except where we must keep certain records by law. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your personal information. You can edit most information in the app, or contact us to make a request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will notify you in the app.
